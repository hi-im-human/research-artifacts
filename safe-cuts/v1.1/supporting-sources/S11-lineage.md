# The process, without rewriting it as the outcome

For what is true now, read [CURRENT-STATUS.md](S11-source-status.md). This catalogue answers a different question: what arguments, experiments, handoffs and checks led to that result?

The originals remain at their existing paths, with their contents and filenames unchanged. They are archived **in place** and ordered here so imports, recorded paths, and relative diagnostic dependencies do not break. The original 01-73 packet numbers remain intact. This L01-L13 sequence is a phase/dependency reading order, not an invented total ordering of concurrent runs. Within a phase, use the original numbered notes, dated receipts and Git history. Files with no recorded time are not assigned one retroactively.

## L01. Choosing the problem

Original Observatory charter (not included), candidate audit (not included), and original work notes (not included), beginning with the September 17 entry. Summer Bee invited System to choose a problem; System chose Durer and later a smaller safe-cut target. The research did not begin as a commission to repair a thesis.

## L02. The target and source discrepancy

Durer Edge-Unfolding (not included) notes 01-07, the source ledger, and the preserved private correspondence record. These document target selection, the thesis claim, the citation comparison, an earlier mistaken source reading and its correction. A historical statement that a problem was open or that no correction was found is a dated report, not a renewed present-day search result. Private correspondence is not public endorsement.

## L03. The original conditional bridge

Numbered notes 08-37 in the same directory: exact restriction, hinge uniqueness, face-chain recovery, retained witnesses, finite-seam closure and the first polyhedral adapters. Read a handoff with its return, including PARTIAL results and repaired compiler/API assumptions. Their externally conditional endpoints remain valid as conditional results; do not relabel them general theorems after the later work.

## L04. From real source geometry to the cut surface

Numbered notes 38-73: raw polygons, merged supports, actual maximal facets, cyclic adjacency, all original cuts, source gluing, the quotient, polygon-set reconstruction, and the conditional non-nested closed-band endpoint. Code and receipts stay under their original names. H01-H14 below indexes the retained draft-status comments in this construction lineage. Some reports cover several files, so the header catalogue is not a claim that each file received its own separate run.

## L05. Testing the global argument, not just its angles

Independent 7.15 review (not included) and [middle-window review](S5-small-variation.md). Preserve the distinction between local-angle correctness, unresolved global positional implications, and the later mixed-case existence proof. The stronger every-middle-window-cut claim was not needed for the final existence theorem and is not retrospectively marked solved.

## L06. The mixed mechanism and physical source

Mixed-case formalization (not included) and [physical source bridge](S1-physical-source.md). These separate a locally certified strip mechanism from the subsequent derivation of its inputs from actual polygons. Keep the shifted condition T_k=q_k-sum(q), not the weaker claim that individual q values have both signs. The finished general theorem does not erase this narrower milestone.

## L07. Failed selectors are retained evidence

Uniform-sign residual (not included). This holds the exact failures of the largest-turn selector and the end-panel shortcut, with safe alternatives and the explicit limits of what each counterexample disproves. Neither is a counterexample to the final theorem. Do not drop failed approaches simply because they are absent from the eventual proof.

## L08. Position-sensitive radial sufficient conditions

[Polar-window argument](S4-polar-trace.md) and the radial-gate alternative (not included). Retain the actual affine translations, finite certificate scope and original-versus-trim distinctions. The gate alternative is catalogued as an alternative/superseded route, not assigned a new acceptance verdict. Its separate narrow-window availability problem was superseded by the next mechanism.

## L09. A seam forced by extremal geometry

[Extremal-seam argument and review](S3-extremal-seam.md). The paper implication is R to ordinary safety and RF to a fixed original seam with full safety. That stage did not itself establish universal RF. Keep its initial conditional wording.

## L10. Deriving the remaining support condition

Original rim closure, the folded projection and the full affine circuit supply the required support; the zero-defect branch is separate. The near-singular apparent floating counterexample and exact rejection remain part of the record. Paper acceptance is not relabeled as a Lean run.

## Reconstructing a specific point in the process

The relative links above open the preserved files in the current checkout. For a frozen version, use the recorded commit and path, for example:

```text
git show commit-11:"<lean-project>/GeneralTwoRimEndpoint.lean"
git show commit-14:"<research-record>/<historical-folder>/OPUS-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md"
```

Use `git log --follow -- <path>` to inspect a file's actual revision sequence. A blob ID identifies content, not a creation time or authorship. A Git committer identity alone does not identify the author of every argument. The original signed returns and the contribution record supply that context.

## What this archive can honestly show

It preserves the recorded research process: proposed arguments, reviews, implementation handoffs, code revisions, numerical controls, visible failures and successful receipts. It is not a claim that every temporary file, unrecorded interaction, or internal model computation is captured. In particular, Opus's report includes the scratch-check source and reproduction route; this catalogue does not copy his entire multi-gigabyte temporary build into Git.

Keep originals immutable when describing later truth. A future public package should contain the theorem, relevant proof and reproducibility material without automatically exposing private correspondence or unrelated research. Summer Bee controls release. The archive supports inspection of the work; by itself it is not a novelty certificate, proof of all authorship claims, or permission to publish private material.

This organizational pass ran no Lean compiler and changed no mathematical definition or theorem. The prior desk is preserved at README-before-lineage-643fc1b3.md (not included), using its original blob. The existing thematic shelf map (not included) remains unchanged.
