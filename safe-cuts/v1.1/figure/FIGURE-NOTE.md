The data's `inputs.run` and `inputs.summary` fields are historical source-record **basenames**, not local links. Their complete records belong to the full evidence package, not this rendering component. `run_digest` and the state identities retain their historical values; they have not been reassigned to the privacy-adjusted files.

Every `checks` flag, `claim_outcomes` value, and `fresh_200_bit_enclosure_inside_recorded_boxes` flag is a **copied historical result**. No geometric certificate is regenerated or verified by the renderer.

## Display transformations

The planar vertices are the midpoints of the previously recorded enclosure boxes, preserving normalization N0. The recorded maximum box widths are approximately 4.509×10⁻⁶ for E4 and 1.162×10⁻⁵ for E9. The exact rational widths are retained in the JSON.

The planar panels have equal horizontal and vertical scales. The inset magnification is computed by the renderer and recorded in `out/render-log.json`; it is approximately 28.373 relative to the main E9 panel. The overlap shading is a floating-point clipping of the displayed polygons, for display only. It is not the source of the overlap verdict.

The source-band panel uses an orthographic projection at elevation 28° and azimuth −58°. Horizontal coordinates retain their common scale; vertical coordinates are multiplied by 300,000, as stated in the image. Caps are omitted. Leader lines offset labels on thin faces only. Geometry, witness values, facet order and display settings are unchanged in this derivative.
