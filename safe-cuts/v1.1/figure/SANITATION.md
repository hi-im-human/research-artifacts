# Privacy-adjusted figure derivative

Private source locations were removed from the image footer and accompanying explanatory text. Two source-record locations in the display-data JSON were reduced to basenames. The JSON label now explicitly distinguishes historical evidence from current rendering. The renderer's introductory command was made platform-neutral. Attribution was kept at the public participant-role level; the human handler is Summer Bee.

All material coordinates, developed vertex coordinates, face and hinge labels, seam order, exact witness values, recorded margin bounds, historical verdicts and check flags are preserved. The rendering calculations and display settings are unchanged. Originals and the private correspondence between original and derivative files are preserved outside this public component.

Historical run and state digests continue to identify the original evidence; they are not checksums of the derivative. The new `SHA256SUMS` covers this component's actual files. These packaging checks do not validate the historical exact/interval certificates, rerun Lean, establish the universal theorem or constitute independent peer review.
